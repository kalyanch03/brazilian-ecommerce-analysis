
# Payment Analysis – Olist E-Commerce

## 1. Payment Method Distribution
- Credit card was the most frequently used payment method, appearing in 76,505 unique orders.
- Boleto was the second most frequently used method, with 19,784 unique orders.
- Voucher and debit card payments had significantly lower usage.

## 2. Payment Value Contribution
- Credit cards contributed 78.34% of total payment value.
- Boleto contributed 17.92%.
- Together, these two methods accounted for 96.26% of payment value.

## 3. Credit Card Instalments
- One instalment was the most common option, with 25,407 unique orders.
- Ten-instalment payments contributed approximately R$2.21 million.
- Instalment options ranged from 0 to 24.

## 4. Average Payment Value
- Credit cards had the highest average payment value per order at R$163.94.
- Boleto averaged R$145.03.
- Debit cards averaged R$142.66.
- Vouchers averaged R$98.15.

## 5. Multiple Payment Records
- 96,479 orders had one payment record.
- 2,961 orders had multiple payment records.
- One order in the orders dataset had no corresponding payment record.

## Business Observations
- Credit cards are the dominant payment method.
- Boleto is an important secondary payment method.
- Customers use different instalment options for credit-card payments.
- Multiple payment records per order explain why payment records exceed the number of unique orders.

## Data Source
Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist (Kaggle).
