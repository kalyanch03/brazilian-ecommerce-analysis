5. Business Recommendations

Explore targeted campaigns to encourage one-time customers to make repeat purchases.

Analyse purchasing patterns in the highest-order-volume states to identify opportunities for growth.

Consider loyalty offers and personalised promotions to improve repeat purchasing.

Further investigate customer behaviour by product category and payment method.

6. Conclusion

The analysis indicates that Olist's order volume is concentrated in a small number of Brazilian states, while repeat purchasing is relatively low within the observed dataset period.

Further analysis of products, payments and delivery performance can help identify additional opportunities to improve business performance.
